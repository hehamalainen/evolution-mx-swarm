# Product Requirements Document: AI Understanding Game for Business Executives

## 1. Executive Summary

Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly integral to software development, and yet a significant knowledge gap persists among business executives regarding the unique challenges and methodologies of AI projects. These gaps can result in unrealistic expectations, misaligned goals, and suboptimal decision-making.

This product aims to address that gap by offering a **scenario-based, single-player decision-making tool** designed to educate business executives on the unique aspects of AI in software development. Through engaging scenarios and interactive gameplay, the game will help executives grasp critical differences between traditional and AI-based projects, such as **delivery timelines, defining goals, and managing risks**. With adjustable difficulty levels to accommodate a range of AI knowledge, the game will make learning accessible to all, from AI novices to tech-savvy senior executives.

This game will be offered as a **subscription-based educational platform**, with **custom, evolving scenarios** that allow users to practice defining AI deliverables, estimating timelines, and navigating risks in a low-stakes environment. By focusing on **specific, real-world business scenarios**, this product stands out as a powerful, practical tool for bridging the knowledge gap and empowering executives to lead AI initiatives more effectively.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Business executives often struggle to understand the **unique challenges associated with AI projects**, particularly regarding **timelines, deliverables, and risks**. This lack of understanding often leads to misalignments between business expectations and technical AI processes, resulting in delayed projects, inefficient resource allocation, and missed opportunities. Traditional methods of learning AI concepts (like seminars, books, or online courses) can be dry, unengaging, and lack real-world application.

There is a need for an **interactive, engaging, and practical** tool that allows executives to **experience decision-making within AI project contexts** and provides **immediate feedback** to help them improve their understanding of key concepts. This solution must cater to **users with no prior knowledge of AI** while remaining valuable to those with more experience.

## 3. User Personas

### **Persona 1: Sarah, the Visionary CEO**
- **Background**: 45-year-old CEO of a mid-sized logistics company.
- **Goals**: Leverage AI to improve operational efficiency and reduce costs.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Limited AI knowledge; intimidated by its complexity.
  - Relies heavily on technical teams and lacks confidence when speaking about AI projects in board meetings.
- **Needs**:
  - A simple introduction to AI timelines and deliverables.
  - Decision-making practice in an AI context to confidently align business goals with technical execution.
- **Gaming Habits**: Occasionally plays casual mobile or puzzle games.

### **Persona 2: Mike, the Ambitious CTO**
- **Background**: 38-year-old CTO in the retail sector with technical experience in cloud computing but little exposure to AI.
- **Goals**: Integrate AI features into their e-commerce platform within budget and reasonable timelines.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Struggles to manage expectations of non-technical stakeholders.
  - Unclear on when AI is the right solution compared to traditional software development.
- **Needs**:
  - Insights into identifying realistic deliverables for AI projects.
  - A framework for communicating the nuances of AI timelines to the leadership team.
- **Gaming Habits**: Enjoys strategy and simulation games in spare time.

### **Persona 3: Rachel, the Strategic Consultant**
- **Background**: 50-year-old business consultant specializing in digital transformation.
- **Goals**: Help clients adopt AI efficiently and understand potential business impacts.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Explaining AI project dependencies to executives who expect quick results.
  - Limited real-world data on potential issues and decisions in AI projects to use as examples.
- **Needs**:
  - A practical, hands-on way to demonstrate AI-specific risks and rewards to clients.
  - Scenario-based learning to test her advice against realistic project simulation outcomes.
- **Gaming Habits**: Avid gamer familiar with strategy and simulation games.

## 4. Core Features

### Must-have (P0)
- **Scenario-based decision-making gameplay**:
  - Custom scenarios focusing on planning and managing AI projects.
  - Key differences highlighted between traditional and AI-based projects.
- **Adjustable difficulty levels** to cater to different experience levels.
- **Feedback system** to show the impact of decisions and provide learning points.
- **Progress tracking** through levels and scenario completions.
- **Dynamic consequences**: choices lead to varying success levels based on timeline estimates, resource allocation, and deliverable definitions.
